# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two confirmed audiences, in this order:

1. **Recruiters and employers.** Landed here from a CV, LinkedIn, or a search after an application. Job: confirm Patrick is real, see what he has actually built, and reach GitHub or LinkedIn in one click. Short attention, likely on a laptop, often mid-screening.
2. **Peers and friends.** Fellow students, developers, people met in person. Job: find his links, skim the projects, read the blog casually. Repeat visitors.

Repair customers are not an audience of this site. The repair service has its own site (repair.guba.dev) and this one does not need to route them there.

## Product Purpose

Personal site of Patrick Gheba, Computing Science student at the University of Groningen and Layer 0 datacenter technician (NTT, Google datacenter in Eemshaven). Three surfaces under one domain:

- **Home (`/`)**: who he is, where to find him, where to go next.
- **Projects (`/projects`)**: what he has built, split into Personal and University.
- **Blog (`/blog`)**: project write-ups, things learned, and whatever else is worth writing down.

Success: a recruiter leaves knowing what he has shipped and with his GitHub open; a friend finds what they came for without hunting.

## Positioning

A student site that is candid instead of polished. The projects are real, running, and linked to their source; the voice admits uncertainty rather than performing confidence. Nothing here is a template deliverable or a resume in disguise.

## Operating Context

- Static site: `public_html/` (home) and `projects_html/` are hand-written HTML; `blog_html/` is Hugo output generated from `blog_src/` (`hugo.toml`, custom layouts in `blog_src/layouts/`, posts in `blog_src/content/posts/`). Committed Hugo output is what ships.
- All three surfaces link the same `/style.css`, served from the site root (`public_html/style.css`). Cache-busted with a `?v=YYYYMMDD` query in every page head.
- Served by nginx (`nginx.conf`) in a Docker image (`Dockerfile`, `nginx:alpine`); `docker-compose.yml` runs it locally on port 8080. Pushed to GHCR by GitHub Actions on every push to `main` (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`), deployed to a self-hosted RKE2 cluster via ArgoCD (per the Infra project card).
- No build step for home and projects; edit HTML, commit, push. Blog needs `hugo` run inside `blog_src/` with output committed to `blog_html/`.
- No analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts (fonts self-hosted under `public_html/fonts/`). JavaScript is limited to `public_html/rack.js` (progressive enhancement: rack-unit alignment and the light/dark cabinet switch) plus a two-line inline script that applies the stored cabinet before paint. Every page renders and works without it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home: name, one-line context, two-line intro, social links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram), and a "Go on then" list pointing to Projects, Blog, and Repair.
- Projects: list rows with name, short tag, description, and outbound links (live site, GitHub or GitLab). Six projects: MusicDash, FlashMD, Repair, Infra (Personal); Recipe Recommender, Applied ML (University).
- Blog: list of posts with date and summary; single post pages with a back link. One post so far (Hello World, 6 April 2026). Categories and tags pages are generated by Hugo but not surfaced in navigation.
- Repair link and card **may be removed** from guba.dev; user confirmed this is allowed. The repair website itself (repair.guba.dev, separate GitLab repo) is **out of scope and must not be touched**.
- All other content and links stay factual as-is: project list, social links, university, Groningen footer. Only the user changes facts. Do not add projects, claims, or biographical details that are not already in the repo or confirmed by the user.
- Never state Patrick's year of study anywhere in site copy (user instruction, 2026-09-17). "Computing Science student" is the whole claim.
- Terminology: "Projects" (not "Work" or "Portfolio"), "Blog" (not "Writing" or "Notes"), "Personal" and "University" as the two project groups.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **guba.dev**. Wordmark is the domain in text; no logo asset exists.
- Voice (**binding**): candid, self-deprecating, profanity allowed. The home page line "Absolutely no fucking idea what I'm doing." stays and sets the register. Future copy sounds like a person talking, not a personal brand. No corporate polish, no hustle language.
- Footer signs off with "Groningen".

## Evidence on Hand

- Live deployments: musicdash.guba.dev, flashmd.guba.dev, repair.guba.dev (linked from project cards).
- Source links: GitHub (PatrickGM1) for MusicDash, FlashMD, Recipe Recommender, Applied ML; GitLab (PatrickGM1) for Repair, Infra.
- One blog post: `blog_src/content/posts/hello-world.md`.
- No screenshots, logos, photos, testimonials, or press. Do not fabricate any. Project imagery would have to be supplied by the user.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over pitch.** Every project links to a running instance or its source. If it can't be linked, it isn't listed.
2. **Recruiter in ten seconds.** Name, context, GitHub, LinkedIn, and the project list reachable without scrolling past anything decorative.
3. **Sound like Patrick.** Copy keeps the candid register even when the layout gets more considered.
4. **One stylesheet, three surfaces.** Home, projects, and Hugo blog must read as one site; changes land in `/style.css` and propagate.
5. **Facts are the user's.** Content edits beyond structure and styling wait for the user's word.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was established. Current implementation already provides 44px touch targets on nav, visible focus rings, `aria-current` on active nav, `prefers-reduced-motion` handling, and semantic landmarks; future work keeps at least that baseline.
