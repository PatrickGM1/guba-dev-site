Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

guba.dev is my personal website. It collects as little as possible.

Analytics

The site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It sets no cookies and does not track you across sites. It records anonymous, aggregated hits — page visited, referrer, rough country, browser — with no persistent identifier tied to you. Because nothing on the site identifies an individual, there is no cookie consent banner to click through.

What the site stores on your device

One localStorage value, cabinet, remembers your light/dark preference. It never leaves your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Third parties

Fonts and scripts are served from the site itself. The GoatCounter counter script loads from gc.zgo.at. Outbound links (GitHub, LinkedIn, etc.) take you to those services, which have their own policies.

Your rights

There is no personal data held about you to request, correct, or delete. Questions can go to my GitHub.
